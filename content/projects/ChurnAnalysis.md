Analyzed a B2B SaaS churn dataset covering 566 customers and $1.85M in ARR to identify root causes, segment risk, and surface actionable retention opportunities. Separated avoidable from unavoidable churn — isolating $862K in recoverable ARR driven by competitive losses, unmet feature expectations, and service failures distinct from COVID-19-related shutdowns.
